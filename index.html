Our Story

Where Every Family Feels Beautiful

Glamour A Family Salon was founded with a single vision — to create a space where beauty care is not a luxury, but a warm, inclusive experience for the entire family.

From children's first haircuts to bridal makeovers, we've been Kolkata's trusted destination for premium grooming and wellness. Our team of skilled professionals ensures you leave feeling refreshed, confident, and truly cared for.

Natural & Premium Products We use only certified, skin-friendly products for all treatments
Expert & Trained Staff All our stylists are certified professionals with 3+ years of experience
Hygienic & Safe Environment We maintain the highest standards of cleanliness and sterilization
